# Restaurant Stock System

## Objective

Create a centralized inventory management system for a restaurant.

## Primary Problems

1. Actual stock quantities are difficult to know.
2. Missing inventory cannot be traced easily.
3. Purchasing may be too high or too low.
4. Physical stock counting takes too much time.
5. Inventory information is fragmented across LINE messages.

## V1 Scope

- Authentication
- Users
- Roles and permissions
- Inventory items
- Categories
- Units
- Stock in
- Stock out
- Manual adjustments
- Waste and loss
- Stock counts
- Variance
- Minimum stock
- Low-stock alerts
- Suppliers
- Purchase orders
- Goods receiving
- Dashboard
- Basic reports
- Audit log
- Basic AI insights

## Not in V1

- Recipe / BOM
- Automatic ingredient deduction
- Menu costing
- Advanced forecasting
- Full POS integration
- Autonomous AI actions
